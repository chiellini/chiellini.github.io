I work on computer vision and computational biology. I received my B.Eng. in EIE and B.S. in Finance (dual degrees, 2016–2020) from Huazhong University of Science and Technology, and my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from City University of Hong Kong. My Ph.D. research developed deep-learning pipelines for 3D/4D microscopic image analysis — segmentation, tracking, and lineage analysis of living C. elegans embryos — published at venues including Nature Communications, CVPR, and ICLR. I am currently a Principal Researcher at SIGS, Tsinghua University, and founder of Guangzhou ISAC Artificial Intelligence Limited (HK Tech 300 Seed Funded), developing VLA, WAM, and multi-agent V-LLM systems for specific areas.

Research Interests

  • Computer vision and deep learning — cell segmentation, tracking, and lineage analysis in 3D/4D microscopic images
  • Computational biology — quantitative embryogenesis of C. elegans, live imaging, and molecular quantification
  • Generative models for microscopy — diffusion-based de-noising, super-resolution, and tensor completion
  • Vision-language-action models and embodied manipulation
  • Multi-agent V-LLM systems — VLA and WAM for specific areas
