I work on computer vision and computational biology. My Ph.D. research at City University of Hong Kong developed deep-learning pipelines for 3D/4D microscopic image analysis — segmentation, tracking, and lineage analysis of living C. elegans embryos — published at venues including Nature Communications, CVPR, and ICLR. I am currently a Research Associate at CityU HK, founder of Hong Kong Multi-agentic Chips Design Cooperation (HK Tech 300 Seed Fund team), developing AI-native perception-networking chips, and of AI-Tender-Writer, a multi-agent LLM system for automated tender document generation.

Research and Industry Projects

2025 – Present Hong Kong Multi-agentic Chips Design Cooperation — Founder & Person-in-Charge
A start-up funded by the HK Tech 300 Seed Fund (City University of Hong Kong, 2025 cohort) and the HKSTP Ideation Programme. Developing AI-native perception-networking chips that integrate computer vision and communication on a single system-on-chip, embedding hardware-level dynamic scheduling, ROI-based perception processing, and perception-oriented communication protocols for ultra-low-latency perception sharing across autonomous vehicles, drones, and industrial robots.
2025 – Present AI-Tender-Writer — Founder & Person-in-Charge
A multi-agent AI system integrating large language models, a domain knowledge graph with Graph-RAG retrieval, and reinforcement learning from expert preference to automatically generate regulation-compliant construction tender documents. Outcomes include a U.S. patent application (first inventor) and conference papers under review.
